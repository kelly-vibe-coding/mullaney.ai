# Feed editorial guide

## Pair standard

Ship only clear disagreements about the same claim. Check transcripts. Titles and thumbnails are not enough.

Reject pairs when:

- both clips land on the same side;
- one side only adds nuance;
- the disagreement depends on clickbait wording;
- either clip is blocked from embedding;
- a long-form clip breaks the Shorts experience.

Prefer native Shorts. Recheck any archived clips before restoring them.

## Feed order

- Put the lead first and the counter second.
- Never run the same topic in consecutive pairs.
- Use two waves: one pair per topic first, then interleave repeated topics later.
- Favor theme diversity over several versions of the same argument.
- Keep strong familiar themes, but spread them through the feed.

## Dense themes

These already have good coverage. Add sparingly and defer extra pairs to the second wave.

- Whether learning to code is dead
- Whether prompt engineering is dead
- The AI bubble
- Cursor vs. Claude Code
- ChatGPT vs. Claude

## Themes to hunt

### Models and labs

- OpenAI vs. Anthropic
- Gemini catching up vs. falling behind
- Open vs. closed weights
- Model moats vs. commodities
- Small models vs. frontier models

### Agents and tools

- Agents in production vs. agents as demos
- Computer use ready vs. broken
- MCP vs. CLI
- Memory and RAG solved vs. still a bottleneck
- Multi-agent systems vs. theater

### Safety and governance

- Pause vs. accelerate
- Alignment vs. capabilities as the harder problem
- Regulation helps vs. regulation blocks progress
- Open weights as a danger vs. a requirement
- AI detection works vs. AI detection is dead

### Work and the economy

- White-collar work collapses vs. adapts
- Entry-level hiring ends vs. continues
- AI raises wages vs. flattens wages
- UBI is inevitable vs. a distraction
- Creators get paid vs. creators get replaced

### Education and cognition

- AI makes students smarter vs. creates cognitive debt
- Homework is dead vs. schools adapt
- AI tutoring closes gaps vs. widens them

### Culture

- AI music and film count as art vs. do not
- Style imitation is fair use vs. theft
- Hollywood dies vs. absorbs AI
- AI influencers are authentic enough vs. harmful

### Science and the physical world

- AI discovers science vs. remixes existing work
- Robotaxis are solved vs. not ready
- AI doctors beat humans vs. remain unsafe
- AI helps the grid vs. strains it

### Philosophy

Use these sparingly.

- AGI this decade vs. not close
- Extinction risk is serious vs. hype
- Humans merge with AI vs. stay separate

### Product taste

- Chat remains the main interface vs. agents replace it
- Voice vs. text
- AI companions help vs. harm
- On-device vs. cloud
